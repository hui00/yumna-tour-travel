# Context

Ubiquitous language for the Yumna Tour and Travel website.

## Yumna Tour and Travel

The business, and the only name used in customer-facing language. A tour and
travel operator on Lombok, Indonesia, serving inbound travellers with transport,
tours, rentals, tickets, accommodation booking, and trekking. Positions itself
for Muslim travellers specifically.

Based in Ombe Baru, Kediri, West Lombok Regency, West Nusa Tenggara 83362.
Short form: **Yumna**.

## Yumna

An Arabic-rooted word meaning "the right hand" — the hand you rely on. This is
the brand's trust story and it carries the hero copy. Also present in the
business email address `ajir.yumna@gmail.com`.

## Lampak-Lampak

The name the business is currently listed under on Google
(*LAMPAK-LAMPAK LOMBOK TOUR AND TRAVEL*, 5.0 stars from 6 reviews). From the
Sasak phrase *lampak-lampak jok Lombok*, roughly "let's roam Lombok".

**Not used on the site.** It is a legacy listing name only. Muhajir renames the
Google listing to Yumna Tour and Travel; reviews and rating survive a rename.
Until he does, the listing and the site disagree — and the listing is where
customers come from, so the rename is a launch blocker, not a nice-to-have.

## Muhajir

The operator. Sole named contact for the business, reachable by WhatsApp at
+62 819-1721-6873. Not a team — customer-facing copy must not imply staff that
does not exist.

## Muslim-friendly

The core positioning, and the term used in all customer-facing copy. It resolves
to four concrete services and nothing else: halal dining arrangements, prayer
stops built into tour timing, mosque locations along the route, and
accommodation vetted as suitable. Every use of the term should be traceable to
one of those four.

## Halal

*Permissible.* Correct for a specific thing — halal food, a halal restaurant.

**Not** used as a promise about a whole trip. "Halal travel" is an industry term
implying a standard the business cannot currently evidence: alcohol-free
accommodation, certified catering throughout, prayer facilities, sometimes
segregated pool hours. Nothing here is certified, so the site says
*Muslim-friendly* and names the services.

The word still belongs in page titles, meta descriptions and keywords — "halal
travel Lombok" is what people actually search for. The distinction is between
what the site **promises** (Muslim-friendly, specific services) and what it is
**findable by** (halal).

## Gili

The three small islands off Lombok's north-west coast (Trawangan, Meno, Air).
Reached by **fast boat**. Distinct from the **ferry**, which is the slow
Bali ⇄ Lombok crossing. Both are sold as tickets; they are not the same product.

## Sasak

The indigenous people and language of Lombok. Sold as the **Sasak cultural
tour**, which visits Sasak villages.

## Rinjani

The volcano on Lombok. Sold as guided **trekking** packages of 2D1N and 3D2N.
The highest-commitment, highest-risk product on offer.
